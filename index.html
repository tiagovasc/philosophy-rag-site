PRIVACY & FAIR USE

Simple, without an account.

Your inquiry library

Questions, evidence and answers are saved online for this browser’s random library identifier. Anyone using this browser can view its library. Clearing browser storage loses access to that library; it does not erase the saved server records. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in questions.

Keeping the room available

Daily limits apply to your browser, your shared network and the whole room. The app stores daily protected network fingerprints and usage counts to enforce these limits, rather than full IP addresses. Network changes and privacy tools may affect your allowance. Failed or interrupted research can still consume an allowance because processing may already have started. Reopening a completed saved inquiry does not spend another allowance.

Processing and sources

Railway runs the service and Supabase stores the inquiry library. Answer requests send your question and selected encyclopedia passages to Jev for relevance checks and to OpenAI for synthesis. Hosting and processing providers may retain their standard security and operational logs. Passage-only searches skip both answer services.

AI answers can be incomplete or mistaken. Verify important claims with the linked original entries. This independent tool uses historical SEP and IEP snapshots and is not an official encyclopedia service.
