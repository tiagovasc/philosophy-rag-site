Privacy & usage

Updated 10 October 2026

Philosophy is an independent research tool using saved Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (SEP) and Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy (IEP) content.

What we store

We store submitted questions, retrieved evidence, generated answers, inquiry progress and creation dates. Guests use a random browser identifier. Google sign-in is optional; Supabase Auth stores your Google identity, basic profile and email, and the browser stores the session needed to keep you signed in. We request only identity, email and profile access, with no Gmail or Google Drive access.

Signed-in inquiries are associated with your account through a protected identifier. You can choose to link this browser’s earlier inquiries when signing in. Linked guest inquiries become accessible only to that account, and their original records are preserved. Anyone with access to a signed-in browser can view its library. Sign out on shared devices.

Usage protection

Our application stores daily protected network fingerprints and usage counts to enforce account, browser, shared-network and global limits. It does not store full IP addresses in its usage database or application access-log format. Hosting providers may retain their standard operational and security logs. Failed or interrupted research can consume an allowance because processing may already have started.

Who processes requests

GitHub Pages hosts the frontend. Railway runs the backend. Supabase stores inquiry records and handles sign-in. Google supplies your sign-in identity. For answers, your question and encyclopedia evidence are sent to Jev for relevance checks and to OpenAI through the Codex SDK for synthesis. Passage-only searches skip Jev and synthesis. These providers operate under their own privacy policies and may retain standard processing and security logs.

Retention and your choices

Saved inquiry records and account-library links currently remain stored until the operator removes them; there is no automatic deletion schedule or self-service deletion feature. Clearing browser storage or signing out does not delete server records. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in questions. You can export a completed answer as Markdown.

For access, correction or deletion requests, open a support request to arrange a private contact channel. Do not include personal information, private questions, login tokens or library identifiers in a public issue. We may need to verify library ownership before acting on a request.
